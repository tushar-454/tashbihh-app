1. Purpose and acceptable use

Tashbihh Counter is a Flutter-based Android utility for Tasbih and prayer counting, daily progress, and optional location-based prayer times and Google Drive backup. This website provides information about the app, its privacy practices, and these terms.

Use the app and its connected services lawfully and only with Google accounts and data you are authorized to access. Do not attempt to disrupt services, bypass access controls, or misuse another person’s information. These terms describe use of the app and do not replace any license included with its source code.

2. Prayer-time accuracy

Prayer times are a convenience and may differ from a local mosque’s schedule or your preferred calculation convention. Results depend on location permission, device location and clock accuracy, time-zone settings, the app’s calculation settings, and AlAdhan’s responses.

The app reuses valid cached daily times and otherwise falls back to configured times when synchronization is unavailable. Those fallback times are not guaranteed to fit your location. Location changes within a day may not immediately update a still-valid schedule. Prayer mode’s counting availability follows the schedule being used.

Confirm important prayer times with a trusted local religious authority or timetable. The app does not provide a guarantee of religious or astronomical accuracy.

3. Google Drive backup limitations

Cloud backup is optional and requires a selected Google account, authorization, an internet connection, and available Google Drive service and storage. Automatic checks use a 12-hour due interval while the app is in the foreground; they do not run while it is closed or in the background. Changed progress may therefore remain only on your device until the next successful upload.

Opening Backup/Restore after connecting attempts an immediate backup. Check the app’s status messages to confirm success before switching devices, clearing storage, or uninstalling.

Connecting an account with an existing compatible backup automatically replaces local progress and settings with that backup. Connecting an account without a backup keeps current local progress and creates the first backup. Switching accounts does not remove the previous account’s backup.

The app maintains a latest backup, not a version history or continuous multi-device synchronization service. Backups from different devices using the same account may overwrite one another. You are responsible for checking backup status and preserving important information before actions that replace or delete data. See the privacy policy’s reset and deletion explanation before resetting.
