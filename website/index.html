Location-based times

With your permission, the Android app takes a one-time location reading and sends rounded coordinates to the AlAdhan Prayer Times API to retrieve your daily schedule.

Automatic daily synchronization

While the app is open, it checks whether today’s schedule is valid and refreshes when needed, including after a date or time-zone change. A valid schedule is reused; the app does not continuously track your location.

Location is optional. Without it, configured times remain available but may not match your area. Always confirm important prayer times with a trusted local source.

Keep a copy of your progress

Your account.
Your backup.

Google Drive backup and restoration are optional. No Google account is needed to count.

  1. Choose a Google account

    Open Settings → Backup/Restore and authorize access to the app’s private Drive storage. Unrelated Drive files are outside this permission.

  2. Restore, or start a first backup

    If that account has a compatible backup, its progress and settings replace your local data. If there is no backup, your local progress is kept and a first backup is created.

  3. Back up as you go

    Tap Backup/Restore again for an immediate backup. Automatic checks use a 12-hour due interval while the app is active and upload changed data. They do not run while the app is closed.
